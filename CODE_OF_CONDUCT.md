# Code of conduct

## Our standards

ERP Link aims to provide a respectful, welcoming environment for everyone who participates. We welcome constructive technical disagreement and people of different backgrounds and experience levels.

Participants are expected to be respectful, provide actionable feedback, accept good-faith correction, protect others' privacy, and focus on what benefits the project and community.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexual harassment, publication of another person's private information without permission, and sustained disruptive behavior are unacceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews and other project-managed community spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer through an available private contact channel. Do not publish personal or sensitive details in a public issue. Maintainers should handle reports discreetly, evaluate the context fairly, and take proportionate action, which may include a warning, removal of offending content or restrictions on participation.

Technical security vulnerabilities should instead follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
